# Undo and redo

`ChartHistory` keeps one timeline for everything a user changes on a chart:

- a study added or removed, with its settings, visibility, pane, stacking row and price scale;
- a study's settings, visibility and scale assignment;
- the chart type and the primary series' scale;
- price scale settings: mode, invert, margins, auto-fit, pinned ratio and axis placement;
- panes: moved, folded, resized, added or removed, and brought back with the studies and
  drawings they held;
- drawings, through the drawing controller's own steps;
- the chart settings the settings dialog writes.

They interleave in the order they were made. Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y (and Ctrl+Shift+Z), the rail's
Undo and Redo, and the mobile sheets all walk the same timeline. The widget builds one for its
chart; a host of its own builds one with `new ChartHistory(chart, { draw })` from
`openalgo-charts/widget`.

Try it in the [live example](/examples#one-undo-for-the-whole-chart): add a study, fold its pane,
switch the chart type or draw a line, then walk the steps back and forth.

## How a step is taken back

The chart announces each change it makes, whether a legend button, a dialog, a menu or a
host's own call made it, and the history compares the chart before and after the turn the
change happened in. One user action is one turn, so a study removed with the pane it emptied
is one step. Undo makes the chart look the way it did in exactly the fields that step changed:
it never restores a whole saved state, which would rebuild every study, replay managed data
requests and reset the drawing history. A change the host made since to a neighbouring field,
another study's colour or another pane's height, is still there afterwards.

A pane height or a scale option set in code is a step too: the chart announces
`setPaneWeight`, `setPriceAxisOptions`, `setPriceScaleOptions` and the other setters that had
no event of their own with `layout:change`. A step made of several stretches, a group or a
transaction with an `ignore` inside it, is walked stretch by stretch, each read against the
chart the one before it leaves, so none of them loses its part of the stacking order.

The chart-wide price scale defaults, the mode, invert and margins a pane added later starts
from (`chart.priceScaleDefaults()`), are a field of each step of their own, apart from every
pane's axes. A chart-wide change, from the settings dialog or `setPriceScaleOptions`, is
taken back with them, so a pane added after the undo starts from the old defaults. A change
to one axis from its own menu leaves them alone, on a chart with a single pane too, and is
replayed on that axis alone: undo and redo never write the defaults for it, and never
announce it to linked charts as an appearance change.

Drawings stay the drawing controller's to undo. The history holds each drawing step by the
number the controller reported it under (`historySteps()` and the `step` field of
`drawing:change`) and asks the controller to take it back, so read-only drawings, linked
charts and a host's forced edits keep every rule the controller applies to them. A step the
controller holds under one the history does not (a controller that recorded a host's edit)
is taken back from the drawings either side of it instead, so a press never takes another
drawing with it.

A study input anchor (a price input with `anchor: true`) is a settings patch like any other.
The history takes the steps its drawing controller would record for it
(`delegateInputAnchorSteps`) and records the patch as the drag or `moveInputAnchor` ends, so
each move is one step, undone once and in order with the drawings, and a settings dialog's
**Pick point on chart** is part of that dialog's step. The drawing controller holds none of
them while the history is attached. A host with no chart-wide history walks the drawing
controller's own undo, which holds a point written through the settings, the Pick point, as
a step of its own: the first undo takes the pick back, the next the drag before it, and
neither ever removes a drawing made before them.

A study brought back is re-created under the instance id it had, so the studies reading it and
the alerts naming it find it again. The history tells studies apart by the chart's own object
for each, not by id or kind. If a study the host placed under that id since holds it, of the
same kind or another, the study comes back under a fresh id with the settings the step gives
it: every later step and every study reading it follows it there, and the host's study keeps
the id, its pane and its settings, and no step ever takes it for the one it names.

A pane is a step of its own when it comes or goes with no study bringing or taking it: a
pane left with only drawings and removed with `removePane` comes back with them, and an
empty one comes back empty, each with its height, fold and axes. With nothing plotted to
fit to, its scales get back the range they had at the last recorded change, which is what
places its drawings. A pane holding a series a host plotted is the host's: history neither
removes it nor makes it again, since that would take away or leave behind plotted data. So is
a pane the host made for a primitive of its own (`addPrimitive` at a new index) or for a
drawing it placed there: it is no `pane-add` step, an undo never removes it, and its removal
is no step either, since no undo could give the host its primitive back.

## What history never does

- **It never writes bars.** Undoing a chart type, a study or a pane leaves the price data as it
  is, including a bar that arrived after the step was made.
- **It never fires an alert.** A study brought back computes from the bars on the chart and
  reseeds its alerts silently, the way any study added to a loaded chart does; trader alerts
  are not history at all.
- **It never places an order.** Order entry is not a step, and nothing in the history reaches
  an order route.
- **It never reaches another instrument's drawings** (since 2.5.9). When the drawing controller
  loads a whole other document, another symbol's drawings or a restored layout, every drawing
  step recorded before is dropped, with the drawings a removed pane took with it: an undo on
  one symbol never brings back a line of another. The steps' studies, panes and settings stay.
- **It never overrides a study's policy.** A press leaves a study the way its
  [policy](/docs/indicators#study-policies) keeps it: on the chart while `removable: false`, with its settings
  and scales while `configurable: false`, in its pane while `movable: false`. The rest of the
  step still applies. A study that may not move is never moved by a call of its own, but other
  studies still pass it, as the chart lets them: a user's reorder that moves a free study past
  a pinned one is a step and is taken back, and a study removed from above a pinned one comes
  back above it. Two pinned studies never trade places. A step left with nothing to do, such
  as removing a study the host has protected since, is dropped and the press goes on to the
  one before, the way the drawing history treats a read-only drawing, so `canUndo`, `canRedo`
  and the peeks always say what a press would do, and `subscribe` hears when a policy changes
  that. Adding a protected study, and a forced write or remove on one, are the host's and
  never steps.
- **It never removes or weakens a policy the host set later.** A study an undo or a redo
  brings back takes the policy its host holds now, the one the study last had on the chart,
  never an older one the step captured: a restriction the host added after the step was made
  is still there on the study that comes back. A study that left the chart by the host's own
  hand, inside `ignore`, through a forced remove, or left out of a chart the host rebuilt and
  attached, is the host's to bring back: the part of any step that would re-add it is dropped,
  and a step with nothing else to do goes with it.

## Grouping and live edits

A dialog that previews its edits live records one step per session, and a Cancel that puts
every value back leaves none. The widget's chart settings and study settings dialogs do this;
a host builds the same with `group`:

```ts
const end = history.group('Chart settings');
// every change recorded here, observed or transacted, is one step
end();
```

A group that ends as no step takes nothing away: the redo branch is there again after a
Cancel. A whole divider drag or drawing drag is one step without asking, and a study added
while a drawing is dragged is a step of its own. A chart setting (the grid, the status line)
and a scale's auto-fit or pinned ratio are read only when a change is made inside
`transact`, and are recorded that way alone; the widget's context menu runs every row in one.

## The host's own changes

A change the host makes for itself goes through `ignore`, and is never a step: nothing it
does to the chart or its drawings is recorded, no undo or redo takes it back, and the redo
branch is kept. Its drawing edits run through the controller's `untracked`, so every step
already recorded takes them in the way it takes a forced edit. A study it removes is never
brought back by a press, and a command it pushes with `push` is not recorded. Inside a
`transact` or a `group`, the step is recorded on either side of the `ignore` and leaves it
alone.

A listener that reacts while a step is being undone or redone is treated the same way: what
it changes, a drawing included, is its host's reaction and not a step. Loading a layout
starts a new timeline: `widget.restoreState` and `chart.restoreState` clear it.

An appearance change a [linked chart](/docs/chart-linking) applies from another chart is the
other chart's step. Apply it through `ignore`, as the chart grid and the reference host do;
undoing or redoing it on the chart that made it announces the result on `style:change`
again, so the linked charts follow.

A host that rebuilds its chart, for a chart-type switch or a new symbol, attaches the new
chart and drawing controller with `attach(chart, draw)`. Studies are matched by id and panes
by slot, which is what a chart restored from the old one's state keeps, so the steps carry
over; a drawing step the old controller held is then taken back from the drawings either side
of it, and a study the new chart does not hold was left out by the host and is never brought
back. A change the history cannot make itself goes in as a command:

```ts
history.push({
  label: 'Chart type',
  undo: () => rebuildAs('candlestick'),
  redo: () => rebuildAs('line'),
});
```

## When a step cannot be taken back

If a step fails (a study whose descriptor is no longer registered, a command that throws), the
history puts back whatever that press had already changed, drops the step with every step
behind it, and keeps the redo branch, so it never describes a chart that is not on screen.
`onError` reports it; the widget shows a toast.

## API

| Member | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `new ChartHistory(chart, options?)` | `options.draw` joins a drawing controller's steps; `limit` keeps that many steps (100); `series` names the series whose type and scale are history; `setChartType` switches the type through the host; `onError` hears a failed step. |
| `undo()` / `redo()` | Take back the latest step, or apply again the one an undo took back. False when there was none, or it failed. A new action clears the redo branch. |
| `canUndo()` / `canRedo()` | Whether a press would do anything. |
| `peekUndo()` / `peekRedo()` | `{ label?, changes }` for the step a press would take, or null. |
| `transact(fn, label?)` | Record everything `fn` changes as one step. Nested calls join the outer one. |
| `group(label?)` | Merge everything recorded until the returned function runs. |
| `ignore(fn)` | Run a host change that is not a step, drawings included; the redo branch is kept, and a study it removes is never brought back. |
| `push(command)` | Record a host's own reversible step. Inside `ignore` it records nothing. |
| `attach(chart, draw?)` | Follow a rebuilt chart, keeping the timeline. |
| `clear()`, `subscribe(listener)`, `destroy()` | Forget every step; hear every change to what a press would do; stop. |

The widget exposes its timeline as `widget.history` and `ctx.history`. `historyPress(ctx, 'undo')`
and `historyReady(ctx, 'undo')` are what its own controls call; they fall back to the drawing
controller for a custom context built without a history.
