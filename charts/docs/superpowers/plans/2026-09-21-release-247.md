## Constraints and review focus

- Preserve original Charts edits and Claude's worktrees. No OpenAlgo or OpenScript changes.
- No comparison names, emoji or long dashes in added code, comments or documentation.
- Test actual pointer gestures, not only manually emitted drag events.
- A preview does not persist or reset evaluation on every movement; cancellation retains the original threshold.
- Replay, pause, scope changes, removed sources and teardown invalidate obsolete gestures.
- Drag only the selected valid range bound; keep bounds ordered and preserve the other bound.
- Use the source plot's units and current scale, including left or independent axes.
- Drawing-owned levels remain governed by their drawing and do not intercept its drag.
