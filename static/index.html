One platform, many desks

Not just an algo trading platform. A complete trading desk.

Placing orders is only the start. Charting, no-code strategy building, options analytics, scalping and practice trading all run in the same installation, on your own computer or server.

Algo trading on your own server

One broker API and order engine on your own computer or server. Send orders from TradingView, Amibroker, ChartInk, Excel, Python or an AI assistant.

Charting platform

Trade from the chart in the built-in terminal: place and manage orders on the chart itself, with 102 indicators on live market data.

No-code strategy builder

Flow is a drag-and-drop canvas: connect 61 node types, from triggers to indicators, conditions and orders, then switch it on to run by itself.

Options trading platform

18 built-in tools: option chain, Greeks, OI tracker, max pain, volatility surface, GEX, IV smile, straddles and arbitrage, plus portfolio and SIP backtesters.

Scalping platform

A keyboard-driven scalping terminal: arrow keys place CE and PE orders, F6 closes every position and F7 cancels every pending order.

Sandbox testing

Analyzer mode runs any strategy on live market data with 1 crore of practice capital and auto square-off at exchange times, without sending real orders.
