## Plan: NIFTY Weekly Momentum Strategy

Build a local-only Lean live strategy whose signal is derived from OpenAlgo mode-3 depth data for a fixed, common-expiry basket of NIFTY constituent single-stock futures. The source weights come from the attached 2026-07-31 CSV; its 49 rows cover 97.97% of the index and are normalized once after premarket futures-eligibility checks, never dynamically by tick activity. OpenAlgo's NFO instrument metadata resolves each actual dated contract and broker symbol/token; no futures symbol is constructed from ticker text. These futures are data-only and can never generate orders. The traded v1 instrument is the exact at-the-money NIFTY option from W+1, defined as the second distinct exchange-listed expiry, admitted only when IV, projected carry, spread, and all-in costs are acceptable. Current-expiry/W0 and ITM fallback options are excluded. A W+1 defined-risk debit spread remains a later feature only after multi-leg reconciliation passes. The design uses strict data-quality gates, bounded limit-order execution, dynamic broker lot sizing, and a strategy-level 2% daily loss ceiling. Because no historical depth dataset exists, implementation proceeds through recording, deterministic replay, shadow trading, and one-lot live gates before the full risk budget is enabled.

### Phase 2: Stock-Futures Z-Score and Session State

13. Implement testable pure-Python components plus a thin `QCAlgorithm` shell: CSV snapshot validator, OpenAlgo NFO instrument resolver, common-expiry selector, fixed futures-universe model, one-second TBT sampler, weighted z-score engine, session state machine, W+1 option valuation/selector, risk ledger, and order manager.
14. Resolve and subscribe before 09:15 IST, then warm the futures signal from 09:15 to 09:20. Every tick updates its contract's state, but each contract contributes at most once to a synchronized one-second frame. Use the one-second trade VWAP when trades occurred and the fresh depth microprice/midpoint otherwise; retain the source flag. Reject a frame with crossed/zero quotes, unresolved sequence gaps, or exchange timestamps outside tolerance.
15. For each fixed eligible future calculate the 30-second log return $r_{i,t}=\log(P_{i,t}/P_{i,t-30s})$ on the same common-expiry contract. The signal is $m_t=\sum_{i\in E}\tilde w_i r_{i,t}$ and $z_t=(m_t-\mu_{300})/\sigma_{300}$ over the trailing 300 valid one-second aggregates. Weights represent NIFTY index influence only: never multiply them by futures lot size, contract price, notional, volume, open interest, or tick count. Weighted breadth remains $b_t=\sum_{i\in E}\tilde w_i\mathbf{1}[r_{i,t}>0]$.
16. Apply strict live coverage gates. All top-10 futures must have valid start/end observations no older than two seconds, and valid contracts must represent at least 95% of the fixed eligible weight. Relative daily weights never change; when coverage is 95-100%, divide the fixed-weight aggregate by measured fresh coverage only to preserve scale and log that adjustment. Below 95%, on any stale top-10 future, or after a sequence gap, mark the frame invalid and suppress entries until 30 seconds of complete valid endpoints have rebuilt.
17. Confirm the stock-futures signal with NIFTY index spot and, when healthy, the read-only NIFTY index future. A bullish candidate initially requires futures-basket $2.0\le z_t\le3.5$, futures breadth at least 65%, NIFTY spot direction agreement, and three consecutive valid frames; bearish rules mirror it. Reject overextension above 3.5 and signal/index divergence. No constituent or index future can be ordered.
18. Use states `DISCOVERY`, `SUBSCRIBING`, `WARMING_UP`, `READY`, `ENTRY_PENDING`, `OPEN`, `EXIT_PENDING`, `COOLDOWN`, `DATA_HALTED`, `RISK_HALTED`, and `FLAT`. Permit one option position, at most three entries daily, a 10-minute cooldown, and no same-direction re-entry until futures-basket z-score resets inside +/-0.5. Stop normal entries at 14:00 and force cancel/flatten by 15:00; on the W0 option-expiry session use a 13:00 last-entry cutoff.

### Phase 3: W+1 ATM Valuation and Execution

19. Resolve W+1 with two broker-backed lookups. Query the canonical NIFTY chain from the session date to obtain W0, then query again from `W0 + 1 calendar day`; OpenAlgo's forward search returns the next actual listed expiry. Require the second result to be strictly later than W0 and within 16 calendar days of the session, cache both dates for the day, and skip trading if either lookup is missing or inconsistent. W+1 means this second distinct maturity even when it is also the exchange's monthly settlement; Lean's US-specific weekly/monthly classifier must not be used.
20. Trade only the W+1 strike identified as ATM by the broker chain, cross-checked against the strike nearest the W+1 forward. Buy its CE for bullish signals or PE for bearish signals. Do not scan or fall back to ITM contracts. Require true BBO no older than one second, visible size for at least one current broker-reported lot, valid tick/lot metadata, and spread no greater than the larger of two ticks or 1.5% of midpoint. If exact ATM fails liquidity or valuation checks, skip rather than moving to a worse strike. Never use LTP as fair value.
21. Derive the W+1 forward from the read-only NIFTY future quote when healthy and cross-check it with same-strike W+1 call-put parity; when futures data is absent, parity is authoritative. Fit a robust W+1 IV smile from liquid surrounding call/put midpoints, solve candidate bid/mid/ask IV with Black-76, and calculate delta, vega, W0/W+1 term structure, realized volatility over matching horizons, and time-of-day IV percentile once at least 20 sessions exist. Reject stale/crossed quotes, parity disagreement, non-convergent IV, or candidate ask IV more than an initial two volatility points above the fitted smile.
22. Estimate holding-period carry by repricing W+1 ATM with 15 and 30 fewer minutes remaining at unchanged forward/IV, then repeat with a conservative IV-crush shock. Initial gates are: midpoint IV/realized-volatility ratio no more than 1.25, IV percentile below 70 when available, projected 30-minute decay no more than 10% of planned trade risk, and spread plus fees plus projected decay no more than 20% of planned trade risk. Require modeled P&L at the basket's calibrated target move to exceed all-in friction by at least 2x. Because ATM premium is predominantly extrinsic, use projected carry and relative IV instead of an intrinsic-value percentage gate. Treat thresholds as forward-test hypotheses.
23. The v1 structure is a single W+1 ATM option. If IV/realized volatility exceeds 1.25, skip the single-leg trade; above 1.60, reject all long-option structures. A later feature may use a W+1 ATM-to-OTM debit vertical, with the short strike nearest the calibrated target, worst executable debit equal to long ask minus short bid, and at least 1.5 reward-to-risk after costs. Enable it only after OpenAlgo `OptionsMultiOrder` per-leg results, partial fills, compensation, restart reconciliation, and maximum unhedged-time tests pass. No futures, synthetic-future, ITM, W0, or current-month fallback order is permitted.
24. Submit W+1 entries as bounded buy limits between midpoint and ask, rounded to the option tick. Reprice no faster than the brokerage's two-second status cadence, at most twice over six seconds, and never beyond the original ask plus the configured 0.5% chase cap. Cancel if signal, valuation, spread, or freshness gates fail. Track intent, cumulative fills, weighted price, remaining quantity, and recovery action. Exit at the first of underlying-signal invalidation, premium stop, target, ten-minute no-progress time stop, 30-minute maximum hold, feed/broker fail-safe, daily kill switch, or 15:00. Mark the long option to executable bid; use a marketable sell limit normally and emergency market fallback only for risk/EOD exits.

### Phase 4: Capital, Fees, and Brokerage Correctness

25. Use the lower of configured strategy capital and available dedicated account equity as session capital. For ₹100,000, set the hard daily loss budget to ₹2,000, stop initiating risk at an estimated net loss of ₹1,800, reserve ₹500 for fees/slippage, and allocate at most ₹500 planned loss to each of three sequential trades. A dedicated broker account is recommended; a shared account also needs an account-wide emergency guard.
26. Size without forcing a minimum lot. For the W+1 ATM option, calculate per-lot risk from the larger of model-based adverse-move loss and premium stop distance, plus conservative IV-crush loss, round-trip fees, and slippage. For the later debit-spread feature, use the smaller of maximum net-debit loss and the tested stop loss, plus legging reserve and costs. Lots are the floor of the minimum allowed by ₹500 trade risk, remaining daily risk, available cash, and a 25% capital debit cap. Skip when one complete dynamic broker lot does not fit; never change strike, violate lot multiples, or tighten a technically valid stop merely to force a trade.

Maintain a strategy-scoped daily ledger using realized fills, bid-marked unrealized P&L, actual fees when available, conservative estimated fees otherwise, slippage, and reserved risk on pending/open orders. Recompute after every quote and order event. At the soft threshold cancel entries and flatten; at/through 2% remain locked until the next session. Document that gaps or disconnections can make an exact 2% fill guarantee impossible.

Do not reuse Lean's old generic `IndiaFeeModel` rates without verification. Reconcile estimates against broker trade-book charges after each session.

Add an explicit `OptionsMultiOrder` adapter for debit spreads that maps each returned leg/order ID into Lean tracking and compensates for rejected or underfilled legs; do not represent the BUY-first broker batch as atomic. Restore same-day state from Object Store, adopt and manage matching strategy-tagged positions, cancel orphan entry tickets, and flatten unknown same-strategy exposure. Require a fresh five-minute signal warmup after restart; never immediately re-enter based on restored z-score values.

**Decisions**

- The attached 2026-07-31 CSV is the source of truth for this version. It has 49 names totaling 97.97%, not 50 names totaling 100%; normalize the fixed eligible futures basket and carry the 2.03 source-gap metric in every run. A later weight file is a reviewed version change, never an automatic website refresh.
- Resolve all constituent futures from OpenAlgo's configured NFO instrument metadata, exactly as the screenshot resolves `RELIANCE25AUG26FUT`. Preserve broker identifiers and use reviewed aliases for genuine renames only; never construct or fuzzy-accept a derivative ticker.
- Use one common monthly expiry across the entire signal basket, selected before market open and frozen for the day. Front month requires at least five full sessions remaining; otherwise use next month. A tradable signal requires at least 90 raw source-weight points mapped and all top-10 names.
- Compute the z-score from synchronized 30-second percentage/log returns of the resolved stock futures using normalized CSV weights. Futures price, lot size, notional, volume, open interest, and tick frequency never alter a constituent's index weight. Runtime entry additionally requires 95% fresh eligible weight and every top-10 future fresh.
- Every constituent and NIFTY future is data-only. Only the exact W+1 ATM NIFTY CE/PE may enter the order whitelist. Use OpenAlgo for both market data and option execution.
- W+1 is the second distinct listed NIFTY option expiry, including monthly settlement when it occupies that slot. If exact ATM is illiquid, stale, overpriced, or too large for one-lot risk, skip; do not substitute ITM, OTM, W0, current-month-by-name, or W+2. A W+1 debit vertical remains disabled until multi-leg recovery passes.
- Earliest option entry is 09:20, normal last entry 14:00, W0-expiry-session last entry 13:00, and mandatory flat time 15:00. Default to three sequential ₹500-risk trades with ₹500 reserved for execution costs/gaps under the ₹2,000 daily ceiling; one position at a time.
- No performance claim or parameter optimization is possible before futures TBT is recorded. Initial z-score, freshness, IV, and exit thresholds are conservative forward-test hypotheses.
