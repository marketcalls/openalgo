# Code of conduct

OpenAlgo is built in the open by traders, developers and brokers. This code sets out how we treat each other
wherever the project gathers, so that anyone can ask a question, report a problem or
send a change without being made to feel unwelcome.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with code and ideas, never with the person.
- Assume good faith. Most mistakes are misunderstandings, so ask before you accuse.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone was new to trading, to this code or to the
  project once.
- Give feedback that can be acted on, and take feedback the same way.
- Credit other people's work, and say plainly when something is not your own.
- Keep other people's data private. Never post anyone's API keys, broker credentials,
  account details, orders or positions, and remove your own at once if you post them
  by mistake.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation or threats, in public or in private.
- Insults, slurs or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background, ability,
  age, appearance, gender, sexual orientation, religion, nationality or level of
  experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their clear consent.
- Promoting market manipulation: pump-and-dump calls, paid tips presented as certain
  profits, or schemes that ask members for money.
- Asking anyone for their credentials, API keys or access to their trading account.
- Spam, repeated off-topic promotion, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Encouraging anyone else to do any of the above.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project gathers: this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions
and reviews, the OpenAlgo community chat, and any event or channel where you speak for
the project.

## Reporting

If something here happens to you, or you see it happen to someone else, email
[rajandran@openalgo.in](mailto:rajandran@openalgo.in). Say what happened, where and when, and include links
or screenshots if you have them. You will get a reply within three working days.

Reports are kept confidential. We share only what is needed to act on a report, and
never the name of the person who made it without their consent.

A security vulnerability is not a conduct report: follow the
[security policy](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens next

The maintainers review every report and choose a response that fits what happened,
from the lightest to the most serious:

1. **Correction.** A private note explaining what was wrong and asking for a change
   of course.
2. **Warning.** A private warning that states the consequence of repeating it, with
   no contact with the people involved for a period.
3. **Temporary ban.** No participation in any project space for a stated time.
4. **Permanent ban.** For sustained or severe harm, including any threat of violence.

Maintainers may also edit, hide or remove comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that break this code. Maintainers are held to the same standard as
everyone else.

## Appeals

If you disagree with a decision about you, write to the same address within 30 days
and say why. Where another maintainer is available, someone other than the person
who made the decision reviews the appeal.
